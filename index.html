Featured Research

Computational Stellar Spectroscopy & Machine Learning

University of Oklahoma • Undergraduate Research
Research Mentors: Dr. Nagaraj Vernekar and Dr. Michael Hayden

Research Goal

My research focuses on developing a neural network for modeling stellar spectra from the physical properties of stars. A primary goal is to create a model capable of effectively working with lower-resolution spectra, such as those observed by DESI, while also enabling the analysis of very large stellar datasets.

By using machine-learning methods, the project aims to significantly reduce the computational time required to model stellar spectra compared with traditional techniques, making it possible to efficiently analyze the millions of stars observed by modern astronomical surveys.

My Work

My work includes preparing and validating large astronomical datasets, developing and training neural networks for stellar spectral modeling, and evaluating model performance using observed stellar spectra.

I have also worked with data from multiple astronomical surveys, including crossmatching APOGEE and DESI observations and preparing observed DESI spectra for independent validation of the model.

Example neural-network predictions for three stars from the validation dataset. The predicted spectra are shown alongside the real spectra across the wavelength range, allowing the model's ability to reproduce both the overall spectral shape and individual spectral features to be evaluated.
